# ⚡ GridMate — Blockchain-Based Peer-to-Peer Energy Trading Platform

> **B.Tech Capstone Project · Group 23 · MIT World Peace University · 2026–2027**

GridMate is a **Blockchain-Based Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Energy Trading Platform** that enables households with rooftop solar generation (**prosumers**) to trade surplus renewable energy with nearby **consumers** through an automated marketplace.

The platform acts as a **digital marketplace, pricing, matching, and blockchain settlement layer over the existing electricity grid**. It does not physically reroute electricity between homes.

## 🎯 Objective

Traditional system:

**Solar Prosumer → Grid → Consumer**

GridMate enables:

**Solar Prosumer → GridMate P2P Marketplace → Consumer**

The system aims to:

- Increase the value received by prosumers for surplus energy.
- Provide consumers with competitive P2P energy prices.
- Automate energy buying and selling.
- Use smart-meter data to identify generation, consumption, surplus, and demand.
- Keep the conventional grid as a fallback when P2P supply is insufficient.
- Provide transparent and verifiable blockchain-based settlement.

## 🔄 Automated Energy Trading

Consumers should not have to manually calculate how much electricity they need every day.
